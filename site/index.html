What it is

scrcpy, without the command line

scrcpy is the open-source tool for mirroring Android over adb. Scrcpy Vwr gives it a home: a live device list, every scrcpy option as a checkbox, and presets you can save and share.

No terminal

Options are checkboxes, grouped and searchable. The app shows the matching scrcpy command if you want to copy it.

Its own scrcpy client

It talks to scrcpy-server directly and draws video in windows the app owns, with a quick actions sheet beside the screen.

Everything bundled

Install one app. The tools it needs ship inside it.

Who it's for

One app, whoever is holding the phone

You don't need to know what adb is to use it. If you do, everything you reach for is one click away.

Plug in your phone and see it on your screen

No commands and nothing else to install. Your phone shows up in the list as soon as it's connected, and a welcome guide gets you started. Type with your keyboard, click with your mouse, and hear your phone's sound.

  • Shows up on its own, no refresh
  • One big Mirror button
  • Connect over Wi-Fi with a QR code
  • Drop an APK or a photo onto the window to send it

Record your phone with one click

Take screenshots and recordings straight from the mirror window. Mark up a screenshot with arrows, boxes and blur, or turn a recording into a GIF you can drop into a chat, a bug report or a tutorial.

  • Screenshot thumbnail you can drag anywhere
  • Markup: pen, arrow, box, circle, pixelate
  • Trim and export GIFs at 320 to 640 px
  • Saves to the folder you choose

The tools you open Android Studio for, beside the screen

The quick actions sheet puts Logcat, a shell, a file browser, an app list and deep links next to the mirrored device. Build your own one-click actions from adb shell commands, or let an AI tool write them.

  • Logcat with tag:, package:, is:crash
  • Open deep links and time cold starts
  • Every scrcpy 5.0 option, with the command shown
  • Emulators start inside Scrcpy Vwr

Test on real devices, no IDE required

Mirror several phones at once and drive them all from one window: taps, swipes and typing go to every device you pick. Grab crash logs, flip dark mode or Wi-Fi in a click, and copy device details straight into a bug report.

  • Send input to many devices at once
  • Toggles for dark mode, Wi-Fi, airplane mode
  • Copy device info as text for tickets
  • Launch, force stop and uninstall apps

Getting started

Mirroring in four steps

  1. Turn on USB debugging

    On the phone, open Developer options and turn on USB debugging.

  2. Connect

    Plug in a USB cable, or pair over Wi-Fi from the Devices screen.

  3. Allow the computer

    Accept the "Allow USB debugging" prompt on the phone.

  4. Mirror

    Double-click the device. Or select it, change options, and press Mirror.
